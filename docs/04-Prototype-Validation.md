## 1. Testing Methodology

- **Method:** Moderated task-based usability testing. A facilitator gives the tester one task at
  a time (see Section 3), observes without helping unless the tester is fully stuck, and records
  what actually happened vs. what was expected.
- **Setting:** In person or over a screen-share call, using the running prototype
  (`index.html` connected to the API, or the static prototype if the API isn't deployed yet).
- **Minimum sample:** 3 real users, covering at least one student, one faculty member, and
  ideally one additional student or admin.
- **Recording:** Note timestamps, hesitations, wrong clicks, and direct quotes where possible
  (with the tester's consent).
